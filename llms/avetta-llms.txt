# Avetta

> Avetta operates Avetta One, a cloud-based supply chain risk management (SCRM) and contractor compliance platform used by 360,000+ businesses across 120+ countries to prequalify, audit, and continuously monitor contractors and suppliers across health & safety, sustainability/ESG, and business risk. Avetta exposes an Open API and ERP connectors through its developer portal so clients can synchronize contractor and supplier data into their internal systems. Backed by Norwest Venture Partners.
