Avetta operates a cloud-based supply chain risk management (SCRM) and contractor compliance platform, Avetta One, used by more than 360,000 businesses across 120+ countries to prequalify, audit, and continuously monitor contractors and suppliers. The platform spans health and safety compliance, prequalification, worker management, sustainability and ESG (GHG reporting, supplier diversity, supplier ESG due diligence), and business risk (financial, cybersecurity, and insurance verification). Avetta exposes an Open API and ERP connectors through a developer portal (docs.api.avetta.com) so client organizations can synchronize contractor and supplier data into Salesforce, Oracle, Workday, ServiceNow, and internal systems. Avetta is a portfolio company of Norwest Venture Partners.

**Areas:** Company · Supply Chain · Risk Management · Compliance · Contractor Management · Safety · ESG · Supplier Management

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for Avetta: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
